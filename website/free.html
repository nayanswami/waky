Cloud

Fast, with your own key

Audio goes to Groq with your key and nowhere else. Waky never stores it. Groq has a free tier; if its limit is reached, Waky can fall back to Whisper on your PC.

Local

Stays on your PC

Download a Whisper model in Settings → Transcription. Speech recognition then runs offline. Smart rewriting and custom modes still need a Groq key.

Private

No audio saved

Recordings are never written to disk. Your key lives in Windows Credential Manager, not in a file.

No account

Nothing to sign up for

No subscription, no trial timer and no Waky account. Download, set a hotkey, start talking.
