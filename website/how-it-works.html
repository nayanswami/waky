How it works

Hold. Speak. Release.

Three moves, no new habits. Everything else happens in the background.

01

Hold the key

Click into any text field, then hold Ctrl + Win. No window opens; a small black pill shows Waky is listening. Prefer hands-free? Tap F9 to start and again to stop, or tap the hold key twice to lock it on.

02

Say it your way

Ramble, pause, say “um”. Switch between Hindi and English mid-sentence. Waky removes the filler, fixes punctuation and keeps your meaning. Say a mode out loud (“write email”, “bullet points”) to shape the result.

03

Let go. It’s written.

The clean text is pasted where your cursor is. Waky never presses Enter, so you read it before you send it. Changed your mind? Press Esc to cancel.

Under the hood

One pass, four stages.

Waky does only what it needs to, in this order, on your PC or through your own key.

  1. Speech to textGroq cloud with your key, or Whisper on your PC.
  2. Clean-upFillers out, wording fixed, vocabulary and corrections applied.
  3. Modes and snippetsSpoken modes, your snippets and per-app profiles.
  4. PasteInto the active field. Never Enter.
