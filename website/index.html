07 / QUESTIONS

Good questions.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC".

The installer is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns about new apps. Click More info, then Run anyway. The guide shows where.

Does it work in every app?

Waky pastes into whichever text field has focus: browsers, Gmail, WhatsApp Web, Notion, editors, chat apps. Windows blocks pasting into apps running as administrator from a normal app.

Why does it keep the microphone open?

So the first word is never clipped. Waky holds the last half second in memory and drops it. You can turn this off in Settings > Audio, and Waky then opens the microphone only while you hold the key.

How do I open settings?

Hold Shift and click the pill, or use the tray icon.

Will Waky start when I turn on my PC?

Yes, by default. It starts quietly in the tray and the pill appears. No window opens. Turn it off in Settings > General.

Can I keep it listening without holding the key?

Yes. Tap your push-to-talk key twice quickly and Waky keeps listening after you let go. Press it once more to stop. You can also use the hands-free toggle key.

Does Waky change my words?

Not unless you ask. The default style, Clean, keeps your own words and only fixes punctuation, fillers and misheard words. Exact words skips the AI entirely and is the fastest. Rewrite is the old, more polished style. Change it in Settings > Output.

How do I fix a word Waky keeps getting wrong?

Open Settings > History (or the tray icon: Review and teach Waky). Pick the dictation, correct the word, and press Teach Waky. It remembers what it heard and what you meant, and fixes it on its own next time. Text only, kept on your PC, and you can switch it off.

Can it write differently in different apps?

Yes. App profiles in Settings let the app you are typing into set the style: casual in Slack or WhatsApp, polished in email, literal in code editors. Presets cover the common apps, and you can add any app.

Can I hide the pill?

Yes. Choose Always, Only while dictating (it fades in when you hold the hotkey), or Always hidden, in Settings > Floating Bar or from the tray menu.

Can I use a mouse button as the hotkey?

Yes. Click a hotkey box in Settings and press your middle button or a side (Back / Forward) button. Combine it with Ctrl, Alt or Shift if you like.

How do updates work?

Waky checks GitHub about once a day and tells you when a new version is ready. One click downloads it, checks the SHA-256, installs in place and restarts. Your settings stay. You can also press Check for updates in Settings > Updates.

Do I need an account?

No. For cloud features you add your own Groq key. For local mode you need nothing.

What about Mac or Linux?

Windows only for now. Other platforms are on the roadmap.
