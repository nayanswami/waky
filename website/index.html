    FAQ
    Good
    questions.
    

    Is it really free?
    Yes. The app is free, with no trial timer and no account. Local Whisper costs nothing, and cloud speech uses your own free Groq key.
    

    Windows says it protected my PC. Is Waky safe?
    SmartScreen warns about every new app that is not code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. You can also verify the SHA-256 of the installer. The steps are in the Install help below.
    

    Does it work offline?
    Yes. Switch to local Whisper in Settings and speech is transcribed on your PC. Clean-up falls back to simple rules without internet.
    

    Where does my audio go?
    Nowhere is it stored. In cloud mode it goes to Groq with your key for transcription. In local mode it never leaves your PC.
    

    Which languages work?
    English and Hindi, including Hinglish mixed in one sentence, plus the many languages Whisper supports.
    

    Mac or Android?
    Not yet. Waky is built for Windows 10 and 11.
