Writing modes

Say what you need. Waky shapes it.

Spoken modes turn one voice note into whatever the moment needs.

01

Email

You say“write email, professional tone — tell Maya I’ll send the deck tomorrow”

Waky writesHi Maya,
I’ll send the deck over tomorrow.
Best, Nayan

02

Notes

You say“bullet points — update the page, ask Maya for copy, share the preview”

Waky writes• Update the page
• Ask Maya for the final copy
• Share the preview

03

Hinglish to English

You say“kal tak bhej dunga, bas final feedback chahiye”

Waky writesI’ll send it by tomorrow. I just need the final feedback.

04

Prompt

You say“prompt mode — product designer, write a launch brief, short sections”

Waky writesRole: Product designer
Task: Write a launch brief
Format: Short sections

05

Rewrite a selection

You saySelect text, hold the key and say “make it shorter”.

Waky writesI think we maybe should push the launch back a little
Let’s push the launch.

06

Your own

You say“standup mode — shipped pricing, working on insights, nothing blocking”

Waky writesYesterday: Shipped pricing
Today: Working on Insights
Blockers: None

Say the mode at the start or end of a sentence. Waky follows it, drops the phrase, and pastes the result. Make your own in Settings → Commands.
